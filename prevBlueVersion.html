About Me

I bridge the gap between business vision and technical delivery — ensuring every sprint ends with clarity, not confusion.

I'm a Project Manager based in India with deep experience supporting end-to-end delivery of a large-scale Real Estate SaaS platform — spanning CRM systems, owner/tenant portals, Android & iOS apps, and inspection tools.

My work lives at the intersection of business requirements, engineering teams, and QA. I own the full cycle: gathering requirements, writing acceptance criteria, managing Jira backlogs, validating workflows, and coordinating deployments — all while keeping stakeholders consistently informed.

Holding a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) gives me a technical foundation that helps me communicate precisely with developers and spot gaps early.
