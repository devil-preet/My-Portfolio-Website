The Person Behind

"I don't just track tasks — I make sure the right things get built, the right way, on time."

I'm a Project Manager and Business Analyst based in India, currently leading delivery of a large-scale Real Estate SaaS platform at Wegile — spanning CRM systems, owner and tenant web portals, Android & iOS applications, property inspection tools, and a backoffice administration system.

My role sits at the exact intersection of client expectations, engineering capabilities, and quality outcomes. I own the full delivery cycle — from gathering requirements and writing user stories, to managing Jira backlogs, validating workflows, and coordinating deployments.

A Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) gives me the technical vocabulary to communicate precisely with developers, spot gaps before they become bugs, and translate business intent into actionable specifications without losing fidelity.
